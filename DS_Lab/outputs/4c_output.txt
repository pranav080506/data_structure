===== PRIORITY QUEUE (LINKED LIST) =====

  Inserted: data=100, priority=p3
  Inserted: data=200, priority=p1
  Inserted: data=300, priority=p4
  Inserted: data=400, priority=p2

After inserting all elements:
  Priority Queue: [200,p1] [400,p2] [100,p3] [300,p4] 

Deleting two elements:
  Deleted : data=200, priority=p1
  Deleted : data=400, priority=p2

Remaining queue:
  Priority Queue: [100,p3] [300,p4]
