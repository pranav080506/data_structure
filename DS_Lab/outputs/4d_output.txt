===== DOUBLE ENDED QUEUE (DEQUE) =====

  InsertRear: 10
  InsertRear: 20
  InsertFront: 5
  InsertFront: 1

After insertRear 10,20 and insertFront 5,1:
  Deque (front -> rear): 1 5 10 20 

DeleteFront and DeleteRear:
  DeleteFront: 1
  DeleteRear: 20

After deletions:
  Deque (front -> rear): 5 10
